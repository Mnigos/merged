Every point is someone else's merge decision.

What counts
Pull requests merged by another person into a repository you don't own. Each one scores log10(stars + 10).
What doesn't
Commits to your own repos, bots by login pattern and list, backdated history. Self-merges count half.
The cap
No single repository can be more than 30% of a score, so a friendly maintainer can't carry you.
The season
Calendar month, recomputed daily from GH Archive. Ranks are projected until the month closes.
